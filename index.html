MISSION BRIEF

() on . Climb the Leonida broadcast tower, reach the giant nest and hurl painted eggs at the sleazy lawyer and his crew below. Each wave gives you 40.0s, and the clock starts once you reach the top.

Controls

  • Climb: A/D or Left/Right arrows (or tap the left/right thirds) to dodge falling crates. Enter or a center tap skips the climb.
  • Aim: mouse/pointer, or A/S/D and arrow keys. Hold click or Space to charge, release to throw. More power means less wind drift.
  • Eggs: 1 / 2 / 3. Upgrades: Q / W / E. Build a 5x streak for Leonida Frenzy: no heavy cooldowns and 2x cash.
