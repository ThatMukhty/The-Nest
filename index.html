THE BRIEF

Golden hour over Leonida. Climb the broadcast tower, dodge the loose struts, reach the nest, then lob spiral eggs at everyone below. Beat the cash quota before the 40.0s clock runs out. The clock only starts once you reach the nest.

  • Climb: A/D, arrows or drag to steer. Enter or tap skips.
  • Aim: mouse or WASD. Hold click or Space to charge, release to throw.
  • Keys 1/2/3 pick eggs. Q/W/E buy upgrades.

TARGETS

  • JEET +$25K. Reverses on near-misses.
  • PAPER HANDS +$50K. Runs from landings.
  • SCAM DEV +$250K. Armored: 2 hits or 1 whale.
  • RUGGER BOSS +$1M. 5 HP, every 3rd wave.
